# Audio credits

Both recordings are from Wikimedia Commons. They are converted to MP3 for
browser compatibility, high-pass filtered at 70 Hz and normalised with
headroom, at their original sample rates. The audio is otherwise unedited.

## pater-noster-spoken.mp3

The Lord's Prayer read in Latin, 28s. Chosen over the alternative Commons
reading, which measured about 15 dB noisier.

- Source: [File:Pater-Noster.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pater-Noster.ogg)
- Author: Dan Palraz
- Licence: **[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)**, public domain dedication

## pater-noster-chant.mp3

The Lord's Prayer sung in Gregorian chant, 75s. Kept in stereo: it is a choir
in a room, and the room is part of it.

- Source: [File:Schola Gregoriana-Pater Noster.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schola_Gregoriana-Pater_Noster.ogg)
- Author: Schola Gregoriana
